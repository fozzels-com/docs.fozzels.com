## Where can I learn how Fozzels works?

Documentation and guides are available at [docs.fozzels.com](https://docs.fozzels.com). Subscription information is covered in the Account & Core Resources section.

## How do I access the Fozzels knowledge base?

No login required — it is publicly accessible at [docs.fozzels.com](https://docs.fozzels.com). Integration guides are under Integration & Connectivity.

## How do I use Mass Actions for content management?

Mass Actions let you quickly confirm, regenerate, and sync content for multiple products at once.

## Is there version history for content changes?

Yes. Fozzels includes version history since release 5.14.

## Which e-commerce platforms does Fozzels support?

Shopify, Shopware 6, WooCommerce, Magento, Lightspeed, and PIM systems such as Katana and Akeneo.

## Which AI models are available in Fozzels?

Multiple models: Claude (Sonnet 4, 4.5), ChatGPT (GPT-5, 5.2), Google Gemini (2.5, 3 Pro), and more. Models are updated regularly.

## I'm switching webshop platforms (e.g. Magento to CCV Shop). What should I do?

Cancel your current subscription, set up the new platform, then re-subscribe with the new integration.
