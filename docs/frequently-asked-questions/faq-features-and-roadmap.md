## Can I set a custom product pull schedule?

Yes. Since release 5.14 you can set a convenient time at both integration and store level.

## Can I customize the suspicious content list?

Yes — customize the suspicious words list to match your preferences.

## Can I embed YouTube videos in generated content?

You can add iframe instructions to your prompt, but Fozzels may filter iframe tags for security reasons. Contact support to discuss.

## Can Fozzels generate content for category pages?

Yes. Category content generation is available — see [How to Create a Content Flow for Categories in Fozzels](/content-creation-flows/how-to-create-a-content-flow-for-categories-in-fozzels).

## Can Fozzels manage merchant feed fields (Gender, Color, MPN)?

Potentially yes, depending on your platform. Contact support with details about your setup.

## Are there notifications for failed imports or errors?

This is a feature request the team is aware of. For now, monitor via the dashboard. Contact support for proactive help.
