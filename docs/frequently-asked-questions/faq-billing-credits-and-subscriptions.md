## My account balance has run out. What should I do?

Log in → check your balance → top up to resume generation. Contact support at support@fozzels.com for assistance.

## How does Fozzels billing work?

It's a balance-based system. Generation consumes AI tokens. Monitor usage in the dashboard and top up before your balance runs out.

## Why is my credit usage / token cost unexpectedly high?

A high volume of completions in one day can spike costs. Check **Transactions** for a breakdown by day and model.

## How is credit usage calculated?

Based on: the AI model used, number of completions, output length, images per prompt, and Web Search usage ($0.07 per search).

## Where can I see a token/credit usage breakdown?

Profile → Transactions shows a detailed breakdown by day, model, input costs, and output costs.

## What are some tips to reduce AI token costs?

Use cost-effective models (e.g. Gemini 2.5), reduce the number of images per prompt, check whether Web Search is enabled, and review Transactions.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Send an email to support@fozzels.com with your cancellation request and desired end date. A confirmation will be sent.

## Can I get a refund for charges after cancellation?

Contact support with your invoice details. They will review and process applicable refunds.

## Why is my invoice higher than expected after a plan upgrade?

Mid-cycle upgrades result in prorated charges (new plan remainder + credit for old plan + next period). Contact support if amounts seem incorrect.

## How do I request a refund or credit note?

Contact support@fozzels.com or info@fozzels.com with your invoice number. They'll review and process refunds if confirmed.

## Can I transfer a token balance between accounts?

Contact support with the source account, destination account, and amount. They can transfer the balance.

## Can I temporarily upgrade then downgrade my plan?

Yes, plans are cancelable monthly. Upgrade for bulk work, then downgrade afterwards.

## Auto top-up isn't working. Why?

Fozzels sends the payment request, but your bank may decline it. Check with your bank and re-add your credit card.

## My balance is lower than expected after a top-up.

Active flows continue consuming tokens. Check Transactions for charges between the top-up and the time you checked.
