## I can't log in to my Fozzels account. What should I do?

Go to [app.fozzels.com/login](https://app.fozzels.com/login) and try the credentials provided by the Fozzels team. If it doesn't work, contact support to verify your credentials and help restore access.

## I get an error when trying to log in via Google. How do I fix this?

Your Google account may not be linked to your Fozzels account. Record a short screen video showing the login process and error, and send it to support@fozzels.com for investigation.

## I get "These credentials do not match our records" when trying to log in.

This usually means the email or password is incorrect. Try the exact credentials provided by Fozzels. If the issue persists, contact support to verify your account and reset your password.

## I lost my password. How do I reset it?

Contact support at support@fozzels.com with your account email address. They can reset your password and send new credentials. Change the temporary password after logging in.

## Fozzels is blocked when I access it from abroad. What should I do?

For security, Fozzels blocks access from most countries outside Europe and the USA. Contact support with your public IP address — they can whitelist it. Alternatively, use a VPN with a European or US server.

## How do I access customer integrations as a reseller?

Log in to your reseller account, go to the "Customers" tab, and click **Impersonate** next to the customer account. To exit, hover over the account widget and click **Leave Impersonate**.

## A former employee's account is linked to our integration. How do I take over?

Contact support to transfer or re-link the account. If you're a reseller, the old account may still be accessible via the Impersonate feature.

## I tried to top up but got an error. What should I do?

Contact support@fozzels.com. They can check the issue on their side and help ensure your account is funded.
