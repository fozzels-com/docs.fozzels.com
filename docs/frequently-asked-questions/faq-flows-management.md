## I accidentally marked a flow as obsolete. Can I undo this?

Contact support — they can reactivate the flow without triggering full regeneration.

## What does the "Obsolete" button do?

It deactivates the flow. It does not auto-regenerate content. Support can reactivate it if needed.

## How do I add products from a specific brand to a flow?

In flow settings → Filters → add a brand filter → use the **IN** operator to include the brand.

## How do I activate a flow?

After creating a flow, activate it in the flow settings. Contact support if you're unsure.

## How do I duplicate flows for multiple stores/domains?

Click the three-dots menu → **Duplicate** → select the target store → adjust name, model, language and links → Save.

## Can I view the batch lists of obsolete flows?

Yes, you should be able to view them. If clicking doesn't work, it may be an already-fixed bug — contact support.

## Should I pause flows during connection issues?

Yes. Suspending flows prevents unnecessary errors and token usage. Reactivate them after support confirms the connection is restored.

## Can I revert auto-generated content (undo sync)?

There is no automatic undo. Duplicate the flow with a corrected prompt and re-run it, or contact support to help reset.
