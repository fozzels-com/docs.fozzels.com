## Overview of the Concept

A Flow is a core concept in Fozzels. It is a custom-configured, multi-step automated process that leverages AI capabilities to transform input data (your product catalog) into ready-to-use, high-quality, targeted content elements (text, images, video) with minimal human intervention.

## Role and Purpose of a Flow

A Flow serves to ensure scalable, controlled, and consistent content generation within your online store.

1.  **Automate** the creation of large volumes of descriptions, meta-tags, images, or videos.

2.  **Ensure** style and tone consistency across different product groups.

3.  **Control** the budget by clearly defining which products and how often content will be generated.

4.  **Synchronize** finished content directly into your integrated store (Shopify, Magento, Next Chapter etc.).

## Key Components of a Flow

Every Flow consists of logical parts that you configure:

### 1\. Product Selection

1.  **Define** the target group of products for processing.

2.  **Use** the powerful filtering system to select products by category, color, SKU, or status.

3.  **Read** more about advanced product filtering [here](/data-import-and-quality/product-filtering-for-content-generation/).

4.  **Result:** Only selected products will be passed to the next stage.

### 2\. AI Instruction (Prompt)

1.  **Write** or **load** detailed instructions for the AI (the prompt).

2.  **Use** dynamic attribute blocks so the AI can adapt content to specific product characteristics (e.g., color, size). **Read** more about attributes and their configuration [here](/data-import-and-quality/attribute-quality-analysis-data-density-percent-custom-attributes/).

3.  **Define** the required style, tone, language, and text limits (e.g., maximum token count).

### 3\. Additional Settings and Control

1.  **Protection against Duplicates:** **Activate** the **Prevent overlapping content generation** feature to avoid redundant content generation for an attribute that has already been processed. **Read** more about preventing overlapping generation [here](/content-creation-flows/prevent-overlapping-content-generation-function-global-prevent-function/).

2.  **Image Optimization:** When working with images, **use** the **Image Resize** feature for automatic optimization of large files. **Read** more about the Image Resize feature [here](/content-creation-flows/ai-configuration-image-optimization-resize-rationale-and-implementation/).

### 4\. Automation

1.  **Configure** the Flow launch schedule (e.g., daily, weekly).

2.  **Set** generation limits (number of products per run).

3.  **Define** synchronization rules (automatically push content to the website or wait for manual confirmation).

## Types of Flows in Fozzels

Fozzels supports three main types of flows, each targeting a specific type of content:

### 3.1. Content Flow

1.  **Purpose:** Generation of text content (product descriptions, short descriptions, meta-tags, SEO texts).

2.  **Target:** Updating a specific product attribute.

### 3.2. Image Flow

1.  **Purpose:** Generation of high-quality images (photo models, scenes, product combinations).

2.  **Target:** Creation of unique visual materials that are synchronized as new product images.

### 3.3. Video Flow

1.  **Purpose:** Generation of video content.

2.  **Target:** Creation of dynamic materials for product promotion.
