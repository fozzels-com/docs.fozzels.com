We are proud to introduce **Fozzels 6.0 -** a massive update where the world’s most powerful AI models become your daily tools, powered by a personalized assistant designed to scale your e-commerce content.

## Meet Jane: Your Intelligent Co-pilot

We’ve integrated **Jane (Beta)**, an AI assistant built to make working with Large Language Models as simple as a standard chat. You no longer need to be a prompt engineering expert - Jane does the heavy lifting for you.

-   **Powered by Claude 4.5 Haiku:** Jane runs on the latest, fastest, and most context-aware model from Anthropic, ensuring near-instant help.

-   **Deep Attribute Integration:** Simply ask Jane to create a prompt using your integration data. She understands your attributes and logic (like `if/else` conditions) and generates the code for you in seconds.

-   **Smart Replace:** Jane can automatically update or replace the code directly within your **Prompt Field**. See the results instantly!

-   **Reference Analysis:** Upload any image via **Attach -** Jane will analyze the style, lighting, and composition to suggest the perfect prompt for recreating that look for your brand.

-   **Beta Note:** Jane is constantly learning. Like any AI model, she may occasionally provide incorrect information, so we recommend reviewing her output for critical tasks.

## Visual Engine: Global Gemini Upgrade

We have completely overhauled our **Google Gemini** model lineup, giving you unprecedented quality and flexibility:

-   **Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash):** High-speed processing for high-volume content generation.

-   **Resolution up to 4K:** You now have full control over image clarity - from standard web quality to professional **4K**.

-   **Custom Image Sizes:** A full suite of aspect ratios for e-commerce, from wide banners to vertical social stories and square product tiles.

## Video AI: Total Upgrade

Video creation on Fozzels reaches cinematic levels with our latest model integrations:

-   **Google Gemini Veo 3.1 (Standard & Fast):** 8-second clips with flawless physics and high detail. Choose **Standard** for quality or **Fast** for rapid iterations.

-   **Grok Imagine Video:** Dynamic video content featuring **native audio generation**.

-   **Advanced Controls:** Toggle resolution and audio settings (**Include Audio**) with a single click.

## Text Content: World-Class Intelligence

Your arsenal of text models now includes the industry's most advanced reasoning engines:

-   **Claude 4.5 Haiku (NEW):** The new standard for speed and human-like natural language generation.

-   **xAI Grok (Lineup):** Access to four new models, including **Grok 4.2 Beta (Reasoning)** for solving complex logical tasks and deep data analysis.

## Stability & Fixes: Making Your Workflow Seamless

We’ve focused on the technical foundations to ensure your experience is rock-solid:

-   **Akeneo Integration:** Fully optimized authorization protocols and stabilized media asset syncing.

-   **Localization:** Fixed SEO attribute synchronization in LangShop for multi-locale storefronts.

-   **Data Integrity:** Enhanced handling of complex attribute syntax and improved **Product Pull** resilience (errors in a single SKU no longer stop the entire sync process).

-   **UI Tweaks:** Fixed state persistence for the "Allow-HTML" checkbox and "Confirmed" content statuses.

### Time to explore!

The 6.0 update is live in your dashboard right now. Not sure where to start? Just **ask Jane -** she’s ready to help you build something amazing!

**To new heights with Fozzels 6.0!**
