Version 5.12 is a landmark update that transforms Fozzels into the central nervous system of your e-commerce operations. We’ve combined the power of multi-model AI with unprecedented integration depth for WordPress and Shopify, ensuring your data works as one unified engine.

## WooCommerce: Data Synergy & Professional SEO

We have elevated the WooCommerce experience by allowing Fozzels to work in perfect harmony with industry-standard data management and SEO tools.

-   **Prompt Data Synergy (The Game-Changer):** For the first time, you can use attributes from third-party plugins directly within your prompts. Create highly specific AI instructions by combining technical variables from **ACF**, focus keywords from **Yoast SEO**, and metadata from **All in One SEO (AIOSEO)** simultaneously.

-   **Advanced Custom Fields (ACF) Support:** Full integration with custom fields. Fozzels now automatically recognizes and synchronizes data into your unique business fields, making the management of niche stores seamless.

-   **SEO Power-Up (Yoast & AIOSEO):** Automate focus keywords, SEO titles, meta descriptions, and social previews (Twitter Cards, Open Graph) directly through Fozzels. Your products are search-optimized the moment they are generated.

## Structural Intelligence: Unified Category Tree

Managing complex store structures is now more transparent and precise. We have redesigned how category hierarchies are handled across multi-store integrations.

-   **Consolidated Category Structure:** Instead of relying on a single store's hierarchy, Fozzels now generates a unified category tree that merges structures from all stores within an integration.

-   **Precision Filtering:** This "global view" allows you to use category filters with 100% confidence - whether you are browsing products or selecting specific collections for your Flows.

-   **Visual Hierarchy:** Access the category structure for each store and locale directly on the Edit Attribute page to ensure perfect alignment with your site's architecture.

## Image Flow 2.0: Multi-Model AI & Full Transparency

Choose the "intellect" that best fits your creative vision. Image Flow now supports flexible AI model selection and provides comprehensive data for every generation.

-   **Vendor & Model Selection:** Switch between industry leaders like **OpenAI** and **Gemini** within your flow settings.

-   **New "Completion Options" Tab:** Get a detailed breakdown for every item in your flow. This new tab, available in both Flow configuration and the Batch list, includes:

-   **Technical Specs:** Time of creation, Image count, Quality settings, and exact Image Size.

-   **AI Context:** AI Vendor, AI Model (with applied filters), and the specific Scenes used.

-   **Source & Result:** A list of used products with their images and the exact **Text Prompt** used for the generation.

-   **Batch List Tracking:** Every generated image is now tagged with the specific model used, allowing for effortless A/B testing and quality comparison within a single workflow.

### System Updates & Process Transparency

We have improved the communication within our interface to ensure you always know exactly what is happening with your data.

-   **Sync Queue Transparency:** We have updated the informational pop-ups for mass actions (**Confirm**, **Regenerate**, and **ReSync**). The system now clearly communicates that synchronization occurs via scheduled cycles (every 4 hours). This helps you know exactly when to expect updates to appear in your store after adding products to the queue.

-   **Shopify: 2026 Standards Compliance:** We have updated our integration engine to meet the mandatory **Shopify Dev Platform** security standards for 2026.

-   _Important:_ New integrations will use the updated secure connection form, while all existing connections established before 2026 will continue to function seamlessly without any action required.

### Bug Fixes & Performance Improvements

-   **Media Quality Optimization:** Fixed an issue where image previews in the Media section were displayed in low resolution. You now get a high-quality preview of the original generated image.

-   **Enhanced Nightly Product Pulls:** We have scaled our technical limits for automated nightly product updates. This ensures 100% reliability for all accounts, regardless of catalog size.

-   **Stable Manual Generations:** Fixed a discrepancy in generation counts during large-scale manual runs. Manual generations now maintain the same consistency and stability as our automated background cycles.

###
Why Version 5.12 Matters

This release breaks down the silos between your data and your AI. By unifying **ACF/SEO attributes**, **multi-vendor AI models**, and **consolidated category trees**, Fozzels 5.12 provides the most stable and powerful automation environment on the market.
