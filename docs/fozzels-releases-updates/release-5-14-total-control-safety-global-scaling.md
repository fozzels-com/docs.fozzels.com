Today’s release is all about your freedom and confidence. We’ve introduced tools that allow Fozzels to work on your schedule, protect your data integrity, and effortlessly scale your business across the globe.

### 1\. Content Time Machine: Revision History & Version Control

Never fear losing a great idea or your original product description again.

-   **Full Version History:** Fozzels now archives every iteration of your content - whether generated by AI or manually edited by your team.

-   **Source of Truth:** The very first version in your history is always the original attribute value we pulled from your store. You always have your "baseline" content just one click away.

-   **GitHub-Style Comparison:** We’ve added a dedicated comparison mode where changes are highlighted. See exactly what was added, removed, or modified between any two versions.

-   **Instant Restore:** Find the version you like best? Simply hit "Apply" to set it as the final result and prepare it for synchronization.

### 2\. Your Business, Your Time: Custom Pull Schedules & Time Zones

We’ve adapted the platform to fit your local rhythm and international market demands.

-   **Custom Pull Schedules:** You are no longer tied to a fixed sync time. Set your data pull frequency at the global integration level or customize it for individual stores — perfect for managing brands across different time zones.

-   **Localized Interface:** Stop calculating UTC offsets. Set your preferred time zone in your profile, and every log, schedule, and timestamp across the Fozzels UI will reflect your local time.

### 3\. Uncompromised Quality: Advanced Suspicious Content Suite

We’ve upgraded our "intellectual shield" to ensure your product pages remain professional and free of AI artifacts.

-   **UI Highlighting:** "Suspicious" phrases or technical AI tags are now highlighted directly in the results for instant spotting and correction.

-   **Custom Trigger Lists:** You define the rules. Customize your own list of unwanted words or constructions at the integration level.

-   **Smart Filtering:** The Daily Report now features a dedicated filter for "Suspicious Content," allowing you to isolate, fix, and sync flagged items in bulk within seconds.
    ![](/img/kb/fozzels-releases-updates/release-5-14-total-control-safety-global-scaling/F2e5Uhg6gBllhtzhjzOHotT0t8KUFWY6kA.png)

### 4\. Global Expansion: WPML Support for WooCommerce

Managing multilingual WordPress sites is now easier than ever.

-   **One-Toggle Setup:** Activate WPML support in your settings, and Fozzels will automatically detect all website locales and languages.

-   **Simultaneous Localization:** Generate unique, localized content for every language version simultaneously within a single integration.
    ![](/img/kb/fozzels-releases-updates/release-5-14-total-control-safety-global-scaling/FUyaZLFhLaA0DvUMHxc6sSnV4I7ciiI54Q.png)

### 5\. SEO Automation: AIOSEO Keywords

We’ve deepened our integration with the **All in One SEO (AIOSEO)** plugin for WooCommerce. Fozzels can now automatically generate and sync **Keywords**. Your full-cycle SEO strategy (Title, Description, and Keywords) is now 100% automated.

### Improvements & Stability

Behind the scenes, we’re ensuring the platform remains future-proof:

-   **Shopify Readiness 2026:** We have proactively updated our infrastructure to ensure full compatibility with the mandatory Shopify API changes effective April 1, 2026. Your stores are safe.

-   **Model Optimization:** Updated integrations for Gemini 2.5 Flash and Nano Banana to align with the latest provider API standards.

-   **Batch Lists Fix:** Resolved a loading issue for "Obsolete Flows" in Batch Lists - managing your historical data is now seamless.
