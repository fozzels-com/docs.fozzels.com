## **Release Highlights**

### **1\. Akeneo PIM Integration**

We’ve expanded our ecosystem to support **Akeneo**, a world-leading Product Information Management system.

-   **Enterprise Scalability:** Seamlessly connect your Akeneo PIM to Fozzels to automate content enrichment for thousands of SKUs while maintaining the integrity of your core product data.

-   **Seamless Sync:** Map complex attribute structures directly to our AI flows for a unified data workflow.
    ![](/img/kb/fozzels-releases-updates/release-5-13-connect-akeneo-optimize-costs-control-quality/RyP45s-aFn544MfTLAtqzhEMIfE7bv9q4w.png)

### **2\. Cost-Efficiency: Intelligent Media Caching**

We’ve re-engineered our image processing architecture to maximize your ROI.

-   **Pay Once, Iterate Often:** Previously, regenerating content could trigger repeated image resizing costs. With our new **Smart Caching**, an image is processed once and reused for all subsequent requests.

-   **Budget Protection:** You are no longer charged for repeated processing of the same asset, regardless of how many times you tweak your text prompts or regenerate descriptions.

### **3\. Enhanced Content Quality & UI**

Managing AI output is now more intuitive and transparent.

-   **Visual Preview by Default:** The "Edit Result" window now renders HTML by default. See your headers, lists, and accordions exactly as they will appear on your storefront.

-   **Suspicious Content Highlighter:** To ensure clean data, the system automatically flags unwanted AI comments or technical tags (e.g., "sorry", "note", "<code>") in orange, pinpointing exactly what needs your attention.

### **4\. Platform Stability & AI Updates**

-   **Stable AI Flows:** We have migrated all systems to the latest stable **Gemini 2.5 Flash** and **Gemini 3 Pro Image** models. This transition guarantees 24/7 reliability and eliminates "Model not found" errors.

-   **Mass Actions:** Manage your Image and Video Flows more efficiently with bulk actions (delete, activate, or change status) directly from the list view.

-   **Shopify URL Precision:** Fixed storefront linking to ensure product URLs always point to the specific store selected in your catalog.
