This week, we focused on one of the most requested features from our WooCommerce community: full support for **Advanced Custom Fields (ACF)**.
![](/img/kb/fozzels-releases-updates/release-5-12-rc1-we-heard-you-woocommerce-acf-support-is-live/z6JN78dr_DHIuf_IaNC5oJxTXh83aqwExg.png)

**Why this is a game-changer:**

-   **Your data, without limits:** Standard WooCommerce fields aren't always enough to tell your product's full story. Now, you can sync any unique data - from complex technical specs to custom marketing labels - directly into Fozzels.

-   **Richer feeds, higher conversions:** More detailed information in your listings means better visibility and higher click-through rates. Use your ACF data to stand out on Google Shopping, Meta, and other marketplaces.

-   **A seamless workflow:** No extra complexity. Fozzels treats your ACF fields as native attributes. You can sync them and  generate AI content for them using the same intuitive flow you already use.

**How to get started?**

Simply toggle the **"Enable ACF"** switch in your integration settings, refresh your product pool, and your custom fields will be ready to work for you!
![](/img/kb/fozzels-releases-updates/release-5-12-rc1-we-heard-you-woocommerce-acf-support-is-live/QYUp9N-xC3deXrXG8AEv4D-tkVdhVXJPgQ.png)
